Ways to join

  1. PhD

    Three to four years of research with a SPERC supervisor, registered through the UPM School of Graduate Studies.

  2. MSc by research

    A research master's on a defined project, often linked to a running grant.

  3. Postdoctoral and visiting researchers

    Researchers with their own funding or a fellowship can be hosted by a member. Recent visitors came from Kazakhstan.

  4. Industry attachment and internships

    Engineers and students from industry can join a project for a set period, for example on a mill trial.

  5. Collaborators

    Universities, agencies and companies can co-supervise, co-apply for grants or join a flagship programme.

Current openings

Funded places change through the year. Write to the supervisor whose work fits yours; they will tell you what is open.
