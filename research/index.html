Research

From waste and biomass to energy, carbon materials and cleaner water: four thrusts along one process chain.

The process chain

  1. FeedstockBiomass profiling and pretreatment
  2. ConversionProcess conversion and optimisation
  3. ProductProduct recovery and development
  4. ImpactTechno-economic and environmental assessment
SPERC performance review 2026, slide 4 (research themes)
T1

Biomass-to-Carbon and Carbon Removal

Locking carbon from farm and mill waste into stable, useful materials.

Focus 2027–2030 Engineered biochar for soil, cement and carbon credits; CO2 capture sorbents from waste; carbon accounting (MRV) for palm and agro-waste.

Group page: Carbon Capture & Biomass Utilisation Research Group (CCBU)

Most-cited work since 2020

  1. Surface functionalization of biochar from oil palm empty fruit bunch through hydrothermal process Processes, 2021; 60 citations
  2. Microwave-assisted Hydrothermal Carbonization for Solid Biofuel Application: A Brief Review Carbon Capture Science and Technology, 2021; 51 citations
  3. Production of biochar from microwave pyrolysis of empty fruit bunch in an alumina susceptor Energy, 2022; 34 citations
T2

Hydrogen from Palm and Agro-Waste

Making hydrogen from what palm oil mills and kitchens throw away.

Focus 2027–2030 Biohydrogen from POME and food waste; gasification and reforming to H2; low-cost catalysts; hydrogen safety.

Group page: Hydrogen Nexus Research Group (HyNeX)

Most-cited work since 2020

  1. Membrane-based electrolysis for hydrogen production: A review Membranes, 2021; 158 citations
  2. Synergistic enhancement of biohydrogen production by supplementing with green synthesized magnetic iron nanoparticles using thermophilic mixed bacteria culture International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 2022; 47 citations
  3. Unraveling the effect of redox potential on dark fermentative hydrogen production Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, 2023; 45 citations
T3

Circular Bioeconomy and Biorefinery

Using microbes, algae and enzymes to turn biomass into products.

Focus 2027–2030 Microalgae biorefinery; enzymes and fermentation products; bio-based materials and natural-fibre composites.

Group page: Biochemical Engineering Advanced Research Group (BEAR)

Most-cited work since 2020

  1. Cultivation of Nannochloropsis sp. microalgae in palm oil mill effluent (POME) media for phycoremediation and biomass production: Effect of microalgae cells with and without beads Journal of Water Process Engineering, 2020; 72 citations
  2. Genetically engineered microalgae for enhanced bioactive compounds Discover Applied Sciences, 2024; 68 citations
  3. Fabrication, characterization and response surface method optimization for quantum efficiency of fluorescent nitrogen-doped carbon dots obtained from carboxymethylcellulose of oil palms empty fruit bunch Chinese Journal of Chemical Engineering, 2020; 51 citations
T4

Water and Waste Resource Recovery

Recovering water, nutrients and materials from waste streams.

Focus 2027–2030 Industrial wastewater and POME to resources (nutrients, water reuse); landfill leachate; plastics and e-waste; emerging contaminants.

Group page: Environmental Technology Research Group (EnTeCH)

Most-cited work since 2020

  1. Co-pyrolysis of biomass and plastic: Circularity of wastes and comprehensive review of synergistic mechanism Results in Engineering, 2023; 227 citations
  2. Recent advances in the application of cellulose derivatives for removal of contaminants from aquatic environments Cellulose, 2021; 80 citations
  3. Low cost and efficient synthesis of magnetic iron oxide/activated sericite nanocomposites for rapid removal of methylene blue and crystal violet dyes Materials Characterization, 2020; 47 citations
P

Digital, Sustainability and Safety Science

Every project is checked for cost, environmental footprint and safety.

Shared platform Every thrust project carries a techno-economic analysis, an LCA and, where data allow, a machine-learning model; process safety for the new hydrogen and bio-processes.
