Research

From waste and biomass to energy, carbon materials and cleaner water: four thrusts along one process chain.

From feedstock to impact

A connected research chain: understand the feedstock, design the conversion, recover useful products and assess the whole system.

  1. 1

    Feedstock

    • Plant biomass

      Palm and agricultural residues; microalgae.

    • Organic waste streams

      Food waste and palm oil mill effluent.

    • Characterise & prepare

      Feedstock profiling, rapid analysis and pretreatment.

  2. 2

    Conversion

    • Thermochemical

      Gasification, pyrolysis and hydrothermal treatment.

    • Biological

      Microalgae, enzymes and fermentation.

    • Chemical

      Catalysis, green solvents and electrolysis.

  3. 3

    Products

    • Gas products

      Biohydrogen, biomethane and bioenergy.

    • Liquid products

      Biofuels, biofertilisers, bioresins and biopesticides.

    • Solid products

      Biochar, biocarbon, bioadsorbents and biocapacitors.

  4. 4

    Impact

    • Energy

      Fuels, power and energy materials.

    • Carbon

      Carbon capture, stable carbon materials and carbon reporting.

    • Circular economy

      Recover resources and return useful materials to the value chain.

Whole-system assessmentProcess optimisation, cost modelling, life-cycle assessment and value-chain analysis connect every stage.
