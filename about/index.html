Sustainable Process Engineering Research Centre

SPERC takes a multidisciplinary, whole-systems approach to technologies and processes, considering environmental, economic, social and sustainability aspects. Its work includes green technology, alternative resources, and the design and operation of systems that use energy and resources more sustainably.

The BEFEW nexus

Biomass, environment, food, energy and water are connected through whole-system analysis and optimisation. Newton Fund programme, 2017–2019.

BEFEW

Whole-system analysis

Biomass

Use renewable agricultural residues as resources.

Energy

Develop solid biofuels and carbon materials for energy storage.

Food

Return biochar to soils for nutrient retention and crop production.

Environment

Retain carbon and develop materials for greenhouse-gas capture.

Water

Use hydrogel–biochar composites to adsorb pollutants in wastewater.

Towards HICoE status

SPERC is working towards recognition as a Higher Institution Centre of Excellence (HICoE) by the Ministry of Higher Education. It assesses its own record against the HICoE R&I Instrument (version 2.1, 2018) and uses the 2027–2030 strategic plan to close the gaps.
