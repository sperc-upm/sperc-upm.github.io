Sustainable Process Engineering Research Centre

SPERC takes a multidisciplinary, whole-systems approach to technologies and processes, considering environmental, economic, social and sustainability aspects. Its work includes green technology, alternative resources, and the design and operation of systems that use energy and resources more sustainably.

Research groups

20 members work in 4 research groups, each led by a group leader.
