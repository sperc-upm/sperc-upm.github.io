CCBU

Carbon Capture & Biomass Utilisation Research Group

CCBUHyNeXBEAREnTeCH

Focus

CCBU researches sustainable biomass valorisation through pretreatment, conversion, product recovery and assessment. It studies environmentally friendly pretreatment, optimised thermochemical and biochemical conversion, and separation and upgrading into biofuels, chemicals and materials. Life-cycle analysis and cost modelling assess the feasibility and sustainability of the whole process.

Research fields

  • Biomass profiling and pretreatment
  • Process conversion and optimisation
  • Product recovery and development
  • Techno-economic and environmental assessment
