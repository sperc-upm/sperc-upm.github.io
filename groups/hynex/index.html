Focus

HyNeX integrates multidisciplinary research in sustainable hydrogen production, advanced material synthesis, hydrogen storage and safety, and end-use applications such as fuel cells and industrial energy systems. These connected fields support low-carbon hydrogen technologies using renewable energy, materials and safety frameworks.
