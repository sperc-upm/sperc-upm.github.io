HyNeX

Hydrogen Nexus Research Group

CCBUHyNeXBEAREnTeCH

Focus

HyNeX produces hydrogen and biogas from palm oil mill effluent, food waste and other agro-residues through dark fermentation, anaerobic digestion and subcritical water processing. From 2027 it adds gasification and reforming to hydrogen, low-cost catalysts and hydrogen safety.

Leads thrust T2 Hydrogen from Palm and Agro-Waste. Group leader: Assoc. Prof. Ir. Ts. Dr. Shamsul Izhar Siajam
