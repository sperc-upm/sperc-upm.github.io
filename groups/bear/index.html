Focus

BEAR integrates biological and chemical principles to design and optimise upstream and downstream processes involving organisms or molecules. Its work includes enzymes, antibiotics, bioenergy and food, bioreactor design, and biotechnology for pharmaceuticals and environmental protection.

Research fields

  • Bioenergy and biofuel
  • Bioproducts and biomaterials
  • Downstream design and scaling up
  • Biosensors
  • Bioremediation
