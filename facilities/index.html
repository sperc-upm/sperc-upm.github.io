Facilities

Laboratories and equipment, arranged along the process from feedstock to environmental testing.

Centre records and flagship proposals 2027–2030 Only equipment in use now is listed. Equipment planned in the flagship proposals is not.

1

Conversion

Thermochemical and biological routes from waste to gas, carbon and bioproducts.

  • Bench-scale gasifier

    Gasification of palm solids to syngas for hydrogen studies.

    HyNeX, Assoc. Prof. Ts. Dr. Nur Syakina Jamali

  • Anaerobic fermenters

    Dark fermentation of POME to biohydrogen.

    HyNeX, Assoc. Prof. Ts. Dr. Nur Syakina Jamali

  • Biochemical Engineering Laboratory

    Departmental laboratory for bioprocess and bioreactor work.

    BEAR

2

Product analysis

Measuring what the processes make.

  • HPLC

    Sugars, volatile fatty acids and ethanol in fermentation broths.

    HyNeX, Assoc. Prof. Ts. Dr. Nur Syakina Jamali

  • Gas chromatography (GC-FID, GC-TCD)

    Composition of product gases and liquids, including hydrogen.

    HyNeX

  • Analytical instrument room

    Shared analytical instruments used by SPERC researchers.

    Shared, Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering

3

Environmental testing

Water, wastewater and emissions.

  • Environmental Engineering Laboratory

    Departmental laboratory for water and wastewater analysis.

    EnTeCH

Related laboratories
