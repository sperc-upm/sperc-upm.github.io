Flagship programmes

Three planned programmes for 2027–2030, built around one palm oil mill and its wastes.

Sustainable Palm and Agro-Waste Valorisation for Carbon Removal, Hydrogen and Circular Bioproducts

SPERC Integrated Flagship Proposals 2027–2030, v2 Proposals under development; aims, work and leadership may change.

Five connected systems. One nexus.

BEFEW links biomass, energy, food, environment and water through whole-system analysis and optimisation. Newton Fund programme, 2017–2019.

BEFEW

A whole-systems approach

Biomass

Use renewable agricultural residues as resources.

Energy

Develop solid biofuels and carbon materials for energy storage.

Food

Return biochar to soils for nutrient retention and crop production.

Environment

Retain carbon and develop materials for greenhouse-gas capture.

Water

Use hydrogel–biochar composites to adsorb pollutants in wastewater.

F1

Planned programme, from 2027 (36 months)

BioCarbon-MYEngineered Palm Biochar for CO₂ Capture and Verified Carbon Removal

BioCarbon-MY turns palm residues into two separate, certifiable products: a shaped sorbent that captures CO₂ from real, humid mill and boiler gas, and a storage-grade char whose carbon removal is measured, verified and counted once.

Aim

Turn palm residues into shaped CO₂ sorbents for real humid gas and storage-grade char with measured, verified carbon removal.

Projects and work packages

  1. 6AEngineered palm biochar and activated carbon: production, activation, doping and scale-up

    6 work packages
    • Characterise palm feedstocks and record how they vary
    • Optimise base biochar production from palm kernel shell and fruit bunches, with yield models
    • Activate and modify the chars, and rank the best variants
    • Shape pellets and test their strength
    • Scale production to trial level and supply the other programmes
    • Build an AI model of char performance and share an open dataset

  2. 6BCO₂ capture under real flue-gas and biogas conditions: humidity, SO₂, cycling and hybrid sorbent–membrane operation

    6 work packages
    • Set up a CO₂ capture test rig
    • Test capture in dry and humid gas and model the results
    • Test tolerance to SO₂ and size guard beds
    • Show durability over repeated capture cycles
    • Trial combined sorbent and membrane capture on real gas streams
    • Feed performance results back into char design

  3. 6CCarbon-removal pathway, MRV, LCA/TEA and durable end use in soil and cement

    6 work packages
    • Check which products meet carbon-removal standards
    • Audit the carbon content of each batch
    • Test biochar in soil
    • Test biochar in cement mortar
    • Keep a shared digital record for measuring and verifying carbon removal
    • Assess costs and life-cycle impacts, and write a policy brief

Partners we are looking for

  • Palm oil mill host for the SPERC Palm Living Lab
  • Energy and oil & gas research (CO2 capture)
  • Utility research (CO2 capture)
  • Cement producers (biochar in cement-based materials)
  • Japanese university / research institute partner

Lead group

Carbon Capture & Biomass Utilisation Research Group (CCBU)

F2

Planned programme, from 2027 (36 months)

PalmH2-LoopIntegrated Biological and Thermochemical Hydrogen from Palm Oil Mill Effluent and Palm Solids, with Gas Cleaning, Process Safety and Techno-Economic Validation

PalmH2-Loop does not presume a winning technology. It measures two routes on the same mill's waste, on one accounting basis, with gas cleaning and safety included, and lets the evidence choose.

Aim

Compare biological and thermochemical hydrogen from matched palm-mill feeds, with common cleaning, safety, cost and carbon evidence.

Projects and work packages

  1. 7APOME biohydrogen and biohythane: immobilised thermophilic dark fermentation and two-stage H₂ + CH₄

    6 work packages
    • Characterise mill effluent and microbial seed
    • Prepare and screen carrier materials for the microbes
    • Optimise hydrogen production by fermentation
    • Show stable long-run operation
    • Combine hydrogen and methane production in two stages and supply effluent to F3
    • Share process data and train postgraduate students

  2. 7BThermochemical hydrogen from palm solids: steam and sorption-enhanced gasification with dolomite and biochar-supported Ni catalysts

    6 work packages
    • Characterise palm solids and design feed blends
    • Prepare the gasification system for steam operation
    • Optimise hydrogen-rich gas from gasification
    • Develop and test catalysts
    • Show catalyst durability and calibrate a process model
    • Supply char to F1, share process data and train postgraduate students

  3. 7CGas cleaning, integration, process safety and TEA/LCA

    5 work packages
    • Set common methods for gas analysis
    • Assess process safety and regulatory needs with the authorities
    • Build and test a gas cleaning train
    • Compare hydrogen routes on cost and life-cycle impact
    • Publish a hydrogen safety guide, train industry and write a policy brief

Partners we are looking for

  • Palm oil mill host with biogas operations
  • Biogas developers
  • Hydrogen producers and offtakers
  • Palm oil regulatory and research agency (data, mill selection)
  • Japanese university partner

Lead group

Hydrogen Nexus Research Group (HyNeX)

F3

Planned programme, from 2027 (36 months)

Palm Circularity HubIntegrated Recovery of Water, Nutrients, Carbon and Bioproducts from Palm Oil Mill Effluent, Landfill Leachate and Mill Sludge

Malaysian mills discharge about 66 Mt of POME a year; this programme aims to build and test, on real Malaysian streams, one affordable integrated train that recovers water meeting the BOD 20 mg/L limit together with nutrients and carbon.

Aim

Recover reusable water, nutrients, bioproducts and hydrochar from POME, leachate and sludge, assessed through a shared mill digital twin.

Projects and work packages

  1. 8AWater Recovery from POME and Landfill Leachate for Boiler-Feed Reuse

    7 work packages
    • Characterise mill effluent and landfill leachate
    • Develop and test adsorbents
    • Optimise membrane bioreactor treatment
    • Design oxidation to remove colour
    • Show an integrated treatment train that produces boiler-feed water
    • Treat landfill leachate with stripping and modified chars
    • Produce IP, papers and a pilot design basis

  2. 8BNutrient and Bioproduct Recovery from Digested POME (Struvite, Microalgae and PHA)

    7 work packages
    • Model nutrient chemistry in digested effluent
    • Recover struvite fertiliser and test it on plants
    • Isolate and screen microalgae strains
    • Grow microalgae with CO₂ captured in F1
    • Harvest microalgae and extract products
    • Explore bioplastic (PHA) from F2 effluent
    • Package the technology and protect IP

  3. 8CSludge-to-Carbon, Digital Mill Twin and Circularity Assessment

    6 work packages
    • Characterise sludge and screen hydrothermal carbonisation
    • Develop modified hydrochar adsorbents
    • Polish treated water with hydrochar
    • Install sensors and data logging
    • Build a digital twin of the mill and assess its circularity
    • Specify the hydrochar product and protect IP
